## 1. Definitions
Terms such as "personal data", "data principal", "Data Fiduciary", "Data Processor", "Consent Manager", "personal data breach" and "Board" have the meanings in the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("Act") and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 ("Rules").

## 2. Scope and purpose
2.1 The Processor processes personal data only to place and record outbound service calls for (a) completion of pending or failed payments and (b) pre-arrival booking confirmation, as described in the order form ("Purposes"), and only on the Fiduciary's documented instructions.
2.2 Categories of data: first name, phone number, preferred language, booking reference, property, city, stay dates, guests, amount due, WhatsApp opt-in record, call audio, transcripts, call outcomes, consent and opt-out records.
2.3 Categories of data principals: the Fiduciary's customers in India and anyone who answers their phone.
2.4 The Processor does not process card data, UPI PINs, OTPs or credentials and redacts them if spoken.

## 3. Processor obligations
3.1 Process only for the Purposes; never for its own purposes, marketing or model training.
3.2 Implement reasonable security safeguards (Schedule 1) including encryption, access control, logging and the measures in the Rules.
3.3 Ensure personnel are bound by confidentiality.
3.4 Assist the Fiduciary with notices, consent records, withdrawal, rights requests (access, correction, erasure, grievance redressal and nomination) within the timelines in Schedule 2.
3.5 Keep a record of processing per Purpose and make it available to the Fiduciary.
3.6 Process and store personal data in India unless the Fiduciary approves a transfer in writing and the transfer is permitted under the Act.

## 4. Sub-processors
4.1 Approved sub-processors are listed in Schedule 3. The Processor gives 30 days' written notice of any addition or change; the Fiduciary may object on reasonable grounds.
4.2 Each sub-processor is bound by obligations no less protective than this Agreement, including no training on the Fiduciary's data.

## 5. Personal data breach
5.1 The Processor notifies the Fiduciary without undue delay and in any event within 24 hours of becoming aware of a breach, with the information then available, and updates it as facts develop.
5.2 The Processor supports the Fiduciary's intimation to affected data principals and its report to the Board (the Rules require a detailed report within 72 hours of the Fiduciary becoming aware), and any CERT-In report within 6 hours where applicable.

## 6. Retention and deletion
6.1 Retention per Purpose as in Schedule 2. Data is erased when the Purpose is served or consent is withdrawn, unless retention is required by law.
6.2 On termination, the Processor returns or deletes personal data within 30 days and certifies deletion.

## 7. Audit
The Processor provides information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance and allows one audit per year (more after a breach) on 30 days' notice, during business hours, subject to confidentiality.

## 8. Significant Data Fiduciary
If the Fiduciary is notified as a Significant Data Fiduciary [CD], the Processor supplies inputs for data protection impact assessments and audits, including logs and model-risk notes.
