# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (template)

**TEMPLATE; REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW.** Not legal advice. Square-bracketed items are to be completed.

This Agreement is made on [date] between **[LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME]**, GSTIN [LEGAL_GSTIN], [LEGAL_ADDRESS] and **[counterparty legal name]**, [address] (each a "Party").

1. **Purpose.** The Parties wish to evaluate a possible engagement for AI-assisted customer service calls (the "Purpose") and may disclose confidential information to each other for it.
2. **Confidential information.** Any non-public information disclosed by a Party, in any form, that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including business plans, pricing, source code, system designs, security information, call recordings and personal data.
3. **Exclusions.** Information that is or becomes public through no fault of the recipient; was lawfully known to the recipient before disclosure; is independently developed without use of the disclosure; or is lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidence.
4. **Obligations.** The recipient will use confidential information only for the Purpose; protect it with at least reasonable care; disclose it only to employees, advisers and contractors who need it for the Purpose and are bound by similar duties; and not reverse engineer any material provided.
5. **Personal data.** Neither Party will share personal data under this Agreement unless a data processing agreement is in place first.
6. **Compelled disclosure.** A recipient may disclose if required by law, after giving prompt notice where lawful and cooperating to limit the disclosure.
7. **Return or destruction.** On request or at the end of the Purpose, the recipient returns or destroys confidential information, except copies kept under a legal retention duty or in routine backups, which stay confidential.
8. **No licence or obligation.** No licence is granted; no Party is obliged to enter any further agreement.
9. **Term.** This Agreement lasts [2] years; confidentiality duties survive for [3] years after it ends, and indefinitely for trade secrets and source code.
10. **Remedies.** Unauthorised disclosure may cause irreparable harm; the discloser may seek injunctive relief in addition to other remedies.
11. **Governing law.** Laws of [India / Singapore]; courts at [city].

Signed: ________________ (for [LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME])  Signed: ________________ (for [counterparty])
