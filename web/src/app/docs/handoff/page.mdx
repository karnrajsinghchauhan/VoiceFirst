# Handoff and CCaaS

A warm transfer always carries a summary of at most five lines, shaped like the client's own case summaries:

1. Who: the booker or a third party, and the language.
2. Booking: masked to the last four characters.
3. What the customer wants.
4. What the AI did.
5. Sentiment, policy references and the next best action.

The record also carries the queue, the reason and SLA fields that mirror the 48 h acknowledgement and 30-day resolution timelines. Handoffs are published to `av.handoffs.v1` for the contact-centre platform to route.

If no human is available, the agent says so plainly and schedules a callback. It never pretends a transfer is happening.

Disputes always go to a human, because a technically correct answer may not be a fair one.
