# Agoda Voice

Agoda Voice places compliant outbound service calls in Hindi, English and Hinglish for two pilot journeys:

- **J1 Payment rescue.** A booking whose payment failed, or is pending past its threshold, gets a disclosed AI call that asks for consent to send an Agoda-hosted payment link over WhatsApp. No answer: one WhatsApp utility template, only if the customer opted in to WhatsApp.
- **J2 Booking confirmation.** Confirms check-in date and time, guests and special requests, answering only from the booking record. It carries no offers (decision D-001).
- **J3 Holdout.** <Spec of="holdout.percent">10%</Spec> of eligible bookings are never called, so lift is measured, not assumed.

ProsperOps runs it as a managed service first; the same Helm chart then runs on the client's own Kubernetes.

## What it never does

- Moves money: no tool can refund, cancel, re-price, issue credit or waive anything.
- Takes card numbers, CVV, UPI PIN, OTP or netbanking credentials by voice.
- Pretends to be human, or keeps a customer from a human.
- Dials without a single-use clearance from the compliance module.

## Phase 2
